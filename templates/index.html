About PCOS

Polycystic ovary syndrome is a common hormonal condition that is manageable with the right care.

Common signs

Irregular or missed periods, excess facial or body hair, acne, thinning scalp hair, weight gain and darkened skin patches.

How it is diagnosed

Doctors usually combine symptoms, blood tests for hormones and an ovarian ultrasound. No single test is enough on its own.

Managing it

Balanced nutrition, regular activity and stress care help, and medication can regulate cycles and symptoms when needed.
